About

the short version

I’m a --‑year‑old developer from Zürich. I got into programming through Minecraft — wanting a server to do something it couldn’t — and never really stopped building tools for other people who want the same thing.

These days that means plugins, a plugin‑building library, and PluginWizard, an attempt at letting people make plugins without writing any code at all.

Almost everything I ship is public. If something of mine is broken, the issue tracker is the fastest way to reach me.
